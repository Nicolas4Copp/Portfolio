Back

Although this was a relatively simple application, its primary purpose was to explore and learn rather than to address an essential need. Despite its simplicity, it resulted in a practical and useful tool for everyday life.

The development process began with creating an initial draft and wireframe in Canva. I then used ChatGPT to analyze my designs and transform my ideas into a structured prompt for Google AI Studio's LLM.

After several iterations, adjustments, and additional coding to refine the AI-generated results, I successfully built and installed a native Android APK on my smartphone through a USB connection.

This project gave me hands-on experience with AI-assisted development, wireframing, prompt engineering, and the process of turning an initial concept into a working mobile application.
